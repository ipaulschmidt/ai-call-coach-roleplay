The user stripped all organization branding from this project after remixing it, and
repeatedly asked for the former names to be gone "from every page in this tool and
database" and "even in the readme file".

- The three banned names are the former organization brands that the practice areas were
  originally named after: the CRM/marketing brand, the paid-media brand, and the
  digital-build brand. Do not reintroduce any of them, and do not propose re-adding them.
- The product is presented generically as **Call Coach**.
- Practice areas are shown as neutral labels: **CRM & Marketing**, **Paid Media**,
  **Digital Build**.
- New scenario, persona, or copy content must be written generically.
- The user plans to connect/share this repository publicly, so committed files (README,
  source, fixtures, notes) are in scope for this rule, not just the rendered UI.
- Third-party product names the tool genuinely trains on (e.g. HubSpot as a practice
  area) are a separate question — ask before broadening the ban to them.
