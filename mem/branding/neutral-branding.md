The user stripped all organization branding from this project after remixing it, and
repeatedly asked for the names to be gone "from every page in this tool and database"
and "even in the readme file".

- Banned words in anything shipped or committed: **SmartBug**, **Point Success Media**
  (and "Point Success"), **Globalia**.
- The product is presented generically as **Call Coach**.
- Practice areas are shown as neutral labels: **CRM & Marketing**, **Paid Media**,
  **Digital Build**.
- New scenario, persona, or copy content must be written generically — do not reintroduce
  org names, and do not propose re-adding them.
- The user plans to connect/share this repository publicly, so committed files (README,
  source, fixtures) are in scope for this rule, not just the rendered UI.
- Third-party product names the tool genuinely trains on (e.g. HubSpot as a practice
  area) are a separate question — ask before broadening the ban to them.
